## Platform

web

## Users

Zarpou serves leisure travelers and corporate travel managers. Leisure travelers plan and book their own trips. Corporate travel managers coordinate employee reservations and billing through the agency workflows.

## Product Purpose

Zarpou is a Portuguese-first online travel agency for planning and booking leisure travel, alongside agency workflows that support corporate travel. The current web application includes flights, hotels, packages, checkout, customer trip management, and administrative tools.

## Positioning

Human support and curated trips are confirmed product differentiators.

## Operating Context

Leisure travelers compare travel options, make reservations, and manage trips. Corporate travel managers work with the agency to coordinate employee reservations and billing. The product is web-based and Portuguese-first.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack: Next.js 15 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS 4.
- The codebase contains public booking and customer-account flows, plus administrative workflows for agency operations.
- Preserve the two audiences and their distinct workflows when extending the product.
- The repository contains claims about installment terms, negotiated fares, 24-hour support, Cadastur registration, and customer reviews. These claims were not confirmed during init; verify them before treating them as durable product facts.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name in the existing application: Zarpou Viagens e Turismo.
- Portuguese-first communication.
- Human support and curated trips.

## Evidence on Hand

The current codebase contains flight, hotel, and package booking flows; customer trip management; and admin workflows. The user confirmed human support and curated trips. No independent supporting evidence for the commercial, registration, service-level, or review claims listed above was provided.

## Product Principles

- Serve leisure travelers and corporate travel managers, with workflows suited to each.
- Keep human support central to the service.
- Curate trips around traveler needs.
- Keep product communication Portuguese-first.
